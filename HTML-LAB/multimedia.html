Flower Seeking Pollinators


Plants, like all living things, reproduce to create future generations. Since plants cannot move, they need ways to transfer pollen from one plant to another. Wind can carry pollen, but insects such as bees and butterflies are more effective because they visit flowers directly. Flowers attract insects through their bright colors, strong scents, pollen, and nectar, which the insects collect for food. As insects move between flowers, they carry pollen with them, allowing fertilization to happen and seeds to form.
